05

Construction & services

Sub-floor
Original bearers milled and reused as blocking between the joists. Structural steel welded and jacked into place.
Termites
H3 treatment throughout (Tom’s wording “H3 oil”, to confirm)
Roof insulation
R4 on the joists plus about 30 mm under the rafters, leaving a void between them. Tom says the roof space stays cool at midday and that this is his own method.
Walls
New insulation around the whole house
Climate
Upstairs: zoned ducted air (exposed duct) and ceiling fans. Downstairs: three-zone split system.
Lighting
CRI 95+ LED in key rooms (most downlights are about 80). LED strips in 45° casings.
