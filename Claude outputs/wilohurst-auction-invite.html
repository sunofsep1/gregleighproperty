The Property
An extraordinary coastal sanctuary designed for those who love to entertain, crowned by a spectacular rooftop terrace capturing uninterrupted 360-degree views across the bay, surrounding islands, hinterland and through to the Gold Coast skyline.
Privately positioned behind secure gates in a quiet street, the home is framed by lush tropical gardens and unfolds across three levels that blend space, sophistication and functionality.
Ground level — a light-filled lounge with built-in wet bar and kitchenette, ideal as a teenage retreat, guest quarters or dual living, flowing to the timber entertaining deck, in-ground pool and tranquil gardens.
Main living level — expansive open-plan living, dining and family areas; a contemporary kitchen with stone benchtops, breakfast bar, oversized walk-in pantry and water views; a wraparound balcony against a bayside backdrop.
Rooftop terrace — breathtaking panoramic views across the bay, islands and hinterland. Sunset gatherings or a quiet sunrise coffee, this space delivers an unforgettable lifestyle.
Accommodation — four generous bedrooms across two levels. The master suite has its own private balcony, walk-through robe, air-conditioning and an ensuite with dual vanities and spa bath. Three further bedrooms with built-in robes on the ground floor, one with private ensuite.
