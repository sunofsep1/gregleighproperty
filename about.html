Meet Greg Leigh

Local knowledge, the global prestige of Sotheby's, and a genuinely personal approach.

Your local specialist

Trusted with your biggest asset

Greg Leigh represents the Redlands and bayside with Queensland Sotheby's International Realty — pairing the reach and prestige of a globally recognised brand with the care, knowledge and accountability of a true local. He knows these streets, the buyers who want to live on them, and exactly what it takes to present a home so it achieves its best possible price.

Greg has called Brisbane home for 17 years, and his love of beautiful homes goes back much further — a fascination since childhood. That genuine passion for architecture, character and craftsmanship is what he brings to every appraisal and every campaign: an eye for what makes a home special, and the drive to make sure the right buyers see it that way too.

Clients consistently describe Greg as upfront, professional and relentlessly communicative — a "property matchmaker" who knows which buyer a home will attract and how to reach them. His approach is refreshingly simple: honest advice, sharp local insight, and communication at every step — no call centres, no pressure, no surprises.

Whether you're ready to sell, weighing your options, or simply curious what your home is worth, the first step is easy and obligation-free: a personalised appraisal from someone who lives and works right here.
