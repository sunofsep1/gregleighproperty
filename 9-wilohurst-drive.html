Privately positioned behind secure gates in a quiet street, the home is framed by lush tropical gardens and unfolds across an impressive three-level design that seamlessly blends space, sophistication and functionality.

Ground Level – The Entertainer's Haven

Designed for versatility, the lower level features a light-filled lounge complete with a built-in wet bar and kitchenette – ideal as a teenage retreat, guest quarters or dual living option. This inviting space flows effortlessly to the outdoor alfresco zone, where a timber entertaining deck overlooks the sparkling in-ground pool and tranquil gardens, all enhanced by refreshing coastal breezes.

Main Living Level – Everyday Elegance

A grand staircase leads to expansive open-plan living, dining and family areas thoughtfully designed to embrace natural light, bay glimpses and cooling breezes. The contemporary kitchen is both stylish and practical, boasting stone benchtops, a breakfast bar, an oversized walk-in pantry and peaceful water views. A wraparound balcony extends the living space outdoors, providing yet another setting to relax or entertain against a beautiful bayside backdrop.

Rooftop Terrace – The Ultimate Viewpoint

Elevating entertaining to extraordinary heights, the private rooftop terrace showcases breathtaking panoramic views across the bay, nearby islands and hinterland. Whether hosting sunset gatherings or enjoying a quiet morning coffee at sunrise, this remarkable space delivers an unforgettable lifestyle experience.

Accommodation

Spanning two levels, the home offers four generously proportioned bedrooms. The master suite is a true retreat, featuring its own private balcony, walk-through robe, air-conditioning and a luxurious ensuite complete with dual vanities and a spa bath. Three additional bedrooms with built-in robes are positioned on the ground floor, including one with a private ensuite – perfect for guests or extended family living.

Additional Features

  • Fully fenced, low-maintenance gardens with tranquil water feature
  • Powder room on the main living level
  • Family bathroom and well-appointed laundry downstairs
  • Remote double garage
  • External blinds and shutters
  • Commercial-grade construction
  • 3-phase power

Lifestyle Location

Positioned just metres from the waterfront at Wilson Park, this bayside sanctuary enjoys exceptional convenience. You are moments from the Red Edge shopping village and cafés, ferry access to the islands, Redland Bay Village shops, local golf course, satellite hospital and waterfront hotel.

Families will appreciate access to a selection of quality primary and secondary schools, while the major retail hub at Victoria Point Shopping Centre is just a short drive away.

This is a rare opportunity to secure an elevated coastal residence that delivers resort-inspired living, exceptional entertaining spaces and breathtaking views – all in one of Redland Bay's most peaceful waterfront pockets.
